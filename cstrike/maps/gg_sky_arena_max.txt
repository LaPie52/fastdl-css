gg_sky_arena_max

This is a medium sized map with very fast game play that takes place in a 3d open maze.  The map has three levels of play.  Buy Zones are included.

-	64 spawn points, 32 per team
-	Nav file for bots
-	Buy Zones
-	Custom skybox texture by Komaokc
